# Editorial policy: project-jekavica-v1

The owner selected a custom contemporary Štokavian register, provisionally
called project Jekavica. It is not an established linguistic standard. Edited
text, labels, metadata prose, quotations in displays and reader exports use
Latin script. Ordinary Latin diacritics remain provisionally. The writing
system is not called bosančica. Source bytes in another script remain unchanged
privately; a supported, reviewed Latin transliteration is required for display.

Preserve the owner's literal forms `svjet`, `vrjeme`, `covik`, `razumit`.
Do not silently substitute `svijet`, `vrijeme`, `čovjek`, `razumjeti`.
`covik` is literal, not approval of `čovik` or a general no-diacritics policy.
Inflections are unresolved. No automatic vocabulary, Ekavian/Ijekavian or
global e-to-ije replacement is approved. The terminology register records
aliases for search and contextual hljeb/kruh proposals; these are not rules.
Keep one coherent vocabulary within a passage. Never invent a theological
difference between hljeb and kruh.

A means technical normalization (NFC and LF in decoded main text). Exact
source bytes, source records and before/after changes remain inspectable.
B means a specifically reviewed mechanical orthographic rule with exceptions;
none is approved yet. C means lexical/grammatical proposals requiring explicit
linguistic and owner review. D means meaning-sensitive, theological or textual
decisions requiring evidence and relevant expert review. The initial software
cannot approve D proposals; they stay blocked. A/B do not confer final passage
approval. No model confidence or consensus is a substitute for review.

Preserve negation, agency, number, tense/aspect where meaningful, conditionals,
commands, quantities, names, geography, kinship, metaphor, repetitions, poetry
and deliberate ambiguity. Do not collapse distinct historical terms without a
documented reason. Automatic flags are reminders with known limits, not a
semantic-fidelity test. They do not identify every changed name or number word.
Review divine names/titles, Spirit/spirit, church/assembly, baptism,
justification, soul/life, hell/underworld and brothers/siblings passage by passage.

Names and titles require independent evidence records. Distinguish an attested
Greek written form from a possible spoken Hebrew/Aramaic form. Mark
reconstructions as such; keep competing forms and limitations. Do not invent
native names, prefix everyone with saint, turn titles into surnames, replace
every Greek title with Hebrew, or provide unsupported pronunciation. No
transliteration scheme, reconstructed name, display mapping or pronunciation
has been approved. The empty name register is intentional.

Separate faithful transcription, owner-approved modernization, evidence-based
explanation, attributed personal reflection and creative adaptation. Do not
harmonize Gospel differences into a purported original, fabricate quotations,
or decide an ambiguous passage from personal preference. Luke 17:21 needs
competing interpretations with evidence in the later pilot, not a preferred
answer filled from model memory. A reference permits inspection, not certainty.
Do not silently remove uncomfortable, institutional, supernatural, judgment,
violent or otherwise difficult passages. Preserve the five complete books when
verified sources become available. Thematic paths must identify their selections
and link complete contexts.

Every final passage needs a real owner's approval of exact text, proposal and
policy hashes. AI critiques are assistance only. Name review, text review and
annotation review are distinct. Changed text invalidates dependent notes and
alignments for review; old decisions remain history. Human owner approval must
never be called professional scholarly verification unless it really was.

Before scaling, review short eligible samples and measure actual human effort.
The examples in `config/terminology.yaml` are invented language samples, not
scripture or approved derived rules. Research, annotation and review packet
types are planned separately; only modernization packets exist in Phase 1.
