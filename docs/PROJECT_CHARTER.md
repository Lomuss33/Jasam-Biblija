# Project charter

Recorded 2026-10-30 from the owner's master prompt v2. The immediate deliverable
is an independently edited modernization of **verified historical source text**,
plus auditable structured data, provenance, review and search/export tools.
Qualified ancient-language translation, if ever commissioned, is a distinct
documented workstream. Credit the historical translators even when attribution
is not legally mandatory.

The independent, personal collection serves readers across Bosnia and
Herzegovina, Croatia, Serbia and Montenegro. Jugoslavenska means shared regional
accessibility. Ekumenska means openness across readers and traditions, not
institutional agreement or church endorsement. Independence does not imply
hostility to faith communities or dismissal of scholarship. The owner's age-of-AI
description supplies no New Age spirituality, reincarnation, manifestation,
esotericism or AI interpretation of scripture.

The owner has approved only GEN, MAT, MRK, LUK, JHN, preserved as complete books
when a source is admitted. This is a selected collection, not a full Bible or a
new endorsed canon. Psalms, Proverbs, Ecclesiastes, James, 1 John, Isaiah,
Thomas and every other writing remain outside it. Cross-references may point
outside without importing wording. Slovenian and Macedonian translations and
Cyrillic reader modes are outside scope.

Language is custom contemporary Štokavian project Jekavica, not an established
standard. Record user forms and unresolved choices before rewriting. Preserve
all passages and their uncomfortable or ambiguous content. Personal interest in
Jesus's path and inner life belongs in attributed reflection, not in a silent
reinterpretation of ancient expressions.

The five data/reader layers are faithful source witnesses, owner-approved
modernization, evidence-based explanation, attributed personal reflection and
labelled creative applications. Keep traditional attribution, named authorship,
anonymous composition, proposed historical dating and competing scholarship
distinct. No fabricated evidence, scholarly endorsement, motives or quotations.

Phase 0 records scope, source-rights findings and policy. Phase 1 is a small
tested local pipeline using synthetic fixtures if no exact source is cleared.
Phase 2 is the five specific pilot passages, with evidence, proposals, names,
alternative readings, separate reflection/story records and measured human
review effort. Phases 3-4 scale only these five complete books and deepen their
knowledge bank. Phase 5 adds justified publication formats from approved data.
No later phase automatically adds books or approves wording.

No model APIs, API keys, paid credits, automated subscription chat sessions,
usage-cap workarounds, cloud infrastructure or public publication are authorized
by implementation. Manual Codex/Gemini subscription packets are the intended
editorial assistance. Preserve exact returned artifacts; chats are not
deterministic rebuild inputs.
