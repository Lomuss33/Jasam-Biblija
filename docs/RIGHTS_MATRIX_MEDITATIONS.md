## Promotion rule

For a row to move from link-only to link-plus-file, record the exact artifact
URL, edition/translator, access date, size, SHA-256, licence/rights evidence,
jurisdiction decision and human reviewer/date. If any required item is missing,
keep the link and the blocker. Do not pass the row to `fetch` or the canonical
source registry.
