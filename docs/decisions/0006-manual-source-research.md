# 0006: Stage source research separately from admission

Date: 2026-10-30. Status: reversible local implementation.

The remaining source questions concern edition ancestry, digital roles and
component evidence. They can be researched before scripture acquisition, but
returned observations must not become source clearance or human review merely
because they are valid JSON.

Add a separate metadata-only manual packet workflow under `jeb sources research`.
Bind its source/dossier snapshots, edition/collection, rights policy, prompt,
response schema and unresolved question manifest. Include no corpus segments or
contact/acquisition permission. Bound packets and returns for practical manual
subscription use, without API/chat automation.

Validate question coverage, exact input identities and reference integrity.
Distinguish known dossier evidence from unreviewed new leads; preserve role and
independence uncertainty. Store each response separately by canonical-content
hash, reject duplicates and produce a readable report with untrusted content
fenced as data. Do not mutate registry, dossier checks, locks, corpus or approvals.

Inspection and any supported dossier indexing remain a separate reviewable step.
The actual archive findings demonstrate this boundary: independently indexed
metadata made the earlier response stale, and a fresh follow-up packet was then
prepared. No source was promoted, no transcriber guessed and no human or legal
sign-off invented. Later passage research/annotation/review formats remain
separate work; this source-metadata packet does not claim to implement them.
