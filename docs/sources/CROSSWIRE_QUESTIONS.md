Subject: Source-edition provenance of SrKDIjekav 1.0 (2017-06-18)

Hello,

I am preparing a small independent modernization project based on an eligible
historical Serbian Bible text, restricted to Genesis and the four canonical
Gospels. The historical translator credits will be retained. Before using any
text, I need to establish the exact digital source and its included components.

The public SrKDIjekav configuration identifies version 1.0 (2017-06-18), OSIS
markup, zText storage and Calvin versification. It declares public-domain
distribution but does not contain TextSource or identify a particular print
edition. Could you point me to existing records answering these questions?

1. Which exact historical edition or earlier digital file was transcribed,
   including publication year/place, publisher, script and stable catalogue or
   facsimile reference? Is there a documented relationship to a specific 1868 edition?
2. Who made the digital transcription or regional conversion, and what later
   corrections, orthographic changes or revisions were applied? The 2017-06-18
   SrKDIjekav upload notice thanks "Br"; who or what does that credit denote and
   what was the role? The separate Ekavian notice credits "Tomas" and is not
   assumed to identify the Ijekavian contributor.
3. Is the original OSIS source available, with its version/history and exact
   adaptation/redistribution permissions?
4. Does the public-domain designation cover only the historical biblical text,
   or also the digital transcription, headings and any notes, introductions,
   apparatus or other components? Are there notices or conditions to preserve?
5. What explains the Calvin versification selection, and are there known verse
   bridges, linked/empty entries, missing text or mapping differences in Genesis,
   Matthew, Mark, Luke or John?
6. Is there an existing export or reconstruction method that preserves those
   links, raw OSIS markup and headings? A pinned synthetic SWORD 1.9.0 test emitted
   linked text through mod2imp without link identity, while mod2osis omitted
   introductions and labelled a Calvin module KJV. A plain-text dump would not
   establish historical fidelity.
7. The configuration says first release, while the automated upload message says
   updated version. Is that generic upload-template wording, or is there an
   earlier beta/source revision with records that can be inspected? A 2014
   development post mentions two unnamed Serbian texts; no link is assumed.

Links to source files, submission records or edition notes would be helpful.
I am asking for provenance and permission records, not an assurance of legal
immunity or worldwide clearance. No text from this module has entered my corpus.

Thank you.
